# 🌍 The Problem

India has a large and diverse population with access to thousands of government schemes, civic services, legal protections, and public resources.

However, **information being available does not mean that information is accessible.**

For many citizens, the real challenge is:

> **"I know there should be a solution, but I don't know where to start."**

Citizens often struggle with:

- Complicated legal and bureaucratic language
- Government information spread across multiple websites and PDFs
- Difficulty understanding eligibility requirements
- Uncertainty about which department to approach
- Difficulty drafting RTI applications or complaints
- Lack of personalized guidance
- Language barriers
- Limited digital literacy
- Difficulty understanding official documents
- Not knowing what action to take after finding information

### The Information Gap

Traditional systems often work like:

```text
Citizen
   ↓
Search Government Website
   ↓
Find PDF
   ↓
Read Complex Information
   ↓
Understand Eligibility
   ↓
Find Correct Department
   ↓
Find Correct Form
   ↓
Figure Out Next Steps
```

For someone with limited digital literacy, this process can become overwhelming.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

## Meet NyayaSaathi

**NyayaSaathi** is an AI-powered civic and legal companion designed around one principle:

> ### Don't just give citizens information. Help them take the right next step.

Instead of forcing users to navigate complicated portals and documents, NyayaSaathi allows them to describe their problem in **simple everyday language**.

For example:

> **"My landlord is not returning my security deposit. What can I do?"**

NyayaSaathi can transform that question into:

```text
Problem Understanding
        ↓
Relevant Rights / Information
        ↓
Required Documents
        ↓
Possible Options
        ↓
Step-by-Step Action Plan
        ↓
Relevant Authority / Application
```

The goal is to convert:

**Confusion → Understanding → Action**

# 🧠 AI Architecture

NyayaSaathi is designed around a **Grounded AI / RAG architecture**.

Instead of relying entirely on the language model's general knowledge, the target architecture retrieves relevant information from trusted sources before generating an answer.

```text
                User
                  │
                  ▼
          Natural Language
             Question
                  │
                  ▼
        ┌──────────────────┐
        │ Intent & Context  │
        │    Detection      │
        └────────┬─────────┘
                 │
                 ▼
        ┌──────────────────┐
        │ Knowledge Search │
        │     / RAG        │
        └────────┬─────────┘
                 │
                 ▼
      ┌──────────────────────┐
      │ Government / Trusted │
      │      Documents       │
      └──────────┬───────────┘
                 │
                 ▼
        ┌──────────────────┐
        │      LLM         │
        │ Response Engine  │
        └────────┬─────────┘
                 │
                 ▼
        ┌──────────────────┐
        │ Action Plan      │
        │ Documents        │
        │ Sources          │
        │ Next Steps       │
        └────────┬─────────┘
                 │
                 ▼
              Citizen
```

### Why RAG?

A civic/legal assistant needs to prioritize:

- Grounded information
- Source traceability
- Reduced hallucination
- Updatable knowledge
- Context-aware responses

The architecture can therefore be updated as government schemes, procedures and official information change.

# 🔐 Responsible AI

Because NyayaSaathi operates in the civic and legal-information space, responsible AI is a core design requirement.

### Ground responses

Prefer information retrieved from trusted and official sources.

### Show sources

Users should be able to understand where important information comes from.

### Avoid overconfidence

The system should clearly communicate uncertainty when information is incomplete.

### Protect users

Sensitive personal information should not be unnecessarily collected or exposed.

### Provide disclaimers

NyayaSaathi provides general informational guidance and does not replace qualified legal professionals.

### Keep humans in control

The final decision always belongs to the citizen and, where necessary, an appropriate professional or government authority.
